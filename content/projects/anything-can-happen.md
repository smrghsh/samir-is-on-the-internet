Anything Can Happen is an interactive projection art piece at the Bounce House in Santa Cruz, where creative code is projection mapped from a tiny computer onto three surfaces across the venue. Melty, psychedelic pieces with a variety of graphics programming techniques exhibit on the main display, and guests play with a section from their phones. I wrote a [creative and technical retrospective](https://vertexshader.substack.com/p/anything-can-happen) on my blog on Substack.
